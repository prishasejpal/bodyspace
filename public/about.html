About BodySpace
DRAFT — not yet reviewed by medical professionals

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 right now. If you are struggling emotionally, call or text 988 (free, 24/7). BodySpace cannot contact emergency services on your behalf.

What BodySpace Is

BodySpace is a free health-education companion built for teens. It's a judgment-free space to ask the health questions you might feel too embarrassed to ask out loud — about sleep, nutrition, exercise, hygiene, and normal adolescent development — explained factually and age-appropriately.

What BodySpace Is NOT

BodySpace is not a doctor and never diagnoses. It cannot prescribe medication, recommend treatment, or replace a visit to a parent, school nurse, or physician. For anything about your own body that worries you, please talk to a real person.

Who Made This

BodySpace was created by a high school student who believes every teen deserves easy access to basic health knowledge, regardless of whether they feel comfortable asking an adult directly.

Draft Status

BodySpace is currently a draft and is pending review and approval by licensed medical professionals before it is made broadly available. Treat responses as a starting point for a conversation with a trusted adult, not a final answer.
